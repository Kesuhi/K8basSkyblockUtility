# **K8bas Skyblock Utility**

A client-side **Hypixel SkyBlock** mod for Minecraft 26.2 (Fabric), developed by Kesuhi.

---

## Features

### General

* Settings screen with search: `/ksu`, `/ksu <text>`, Mod Menu or a key
* HUD editor: `/ksu hud`
* Accent colour, smooth corners and animations
* Keybinds
* Updates from GitHub, installed when you quit after you confirm them: `/ksu update` (`check`, `install <version>`,
  `skip <version>`, `cancel`)

### Highlights

* Mob Highlighter: outlines the mobs you pick, only while in view

### Waypoints

* NPC Search: waypoints with beacon beams at fixed NPCs, outlines on moving ones
* Beam colour per island or per NPC
* "You found" title for Trinity, Tomioka, Duncan, Xalx and Pete

### Mining

* Mineshaft entry alert: corpses, looted count and your keys
* Sack key reminder with a **[Take out n]** button
* Possible corpse spots, hidden once every corpse is looted: `/ksu corpses`
* Organ Donor ding hint, which rules out silent spots

### Fishing

* Bobber Fix: smooth bobber, also on lava
* Fishing Hotspots: ring and filled area in the buff's colour
* Hotspot gone warning: title, sound, chat line or notice

### Odds & Trackers

* Bestiary Tracker: a HUD panel with kills, tiers, kills per hour and the milestone, per profile

Hypixel Mod API. Mod Menu and [Caxton](https://modrinth.com/mod/caxton) (sharper text) are optional. Get the jar
from [Releases](https://github.com/Kesuhi/K8basSkyblockUtility/releases/latest); 1.0.1 was the last 26.1 build.
